The mechanics

Three stages, one clear goal.

Every property on Upalit is held in a dedicated legal entity. Tokens represent an economic interest in that entity — priced against periodic independent valuations, not guesswork.

Stage 01

Choose a property

Browse homes with live token pricing, funding progress, and full valuation history. Pick the one you actually want to live in — not just an index.

Stage 02

Buy tokens over time

Purchase tokens on your own schedule. Your stake is tracked continuously against the property's independently appraised value.

Stage 03

Reach 30%, apply to finance the rest

Once your holdings reach 30% of the property's value, you're eligible to apply for financing on the remaining 70% through our lending partners. Eligibility isn't a guarantee of approval.

Held to a higher bar

Every property sits inside its own legal entity.

Upalit is structured so your token reflects a real, defensible economic interest — not a marketing term.

Independent valuation

Each property is appraised on a fixed schedule by a licensed, independent valuer. Token price is never set internally.

Segregated ownership

Each property is held through its own dedicated entity, keeping it legally separate from Upalit's own balance sheet.

Verified investors only

Every account passes identity and eligibility checks before it can hold or trade tokens, in line with applicable regulation.

Risk disclosure — property values can fall as well as rise. Tokens may be less liquid than the secondary market suggests. Reaching 30% makes you eligible to apply for financing; it is not an approval or guarantee. Full offering documents and jurisdiction-specific disclosures are provided before any purchase.
